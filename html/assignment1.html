Know How ITM Skills is Revolutionizing Education Industry

ITM Group of Institutions brings its 34 years leadership in higher education, skill development and employment generation under a single umbrella through the launch of ITM Skills University. Over the last 3 decades, ITM Group has grown into a multi-disciplinary, employment focused higher education brand, offering diverse skill and technology focused graduate and undergraduate programs. Our legacy started with ITM Business School in 1991, and since then we have built some of Mumbai’s most iconic institutions- ITM Institute of Hotel Management, ITM Institute of Design and Media, ITM Institute of Health Sciences, ITM Executive Education and more. Over the last 15 years, ITM has emerged as one of India’s premier skill development institutions with ITM Skills Academy, having trained and placed over 2,80,000 people into the BFSI, Healthcare and Retail Sectors.
